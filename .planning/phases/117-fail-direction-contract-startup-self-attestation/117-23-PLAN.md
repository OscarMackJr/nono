<objective>
Close WR-07: `AppliedAncestorTraverseGuard::application()` and
`AppliedAncestorReadAttributesGuard::application()` (both fixed for NR3-02 by Plan 117-14) are
two-valued functions of `self.applied.is_empty()` — they can only ever return `NotApplicable` or
`Applied`, never `PartiallyApplied` or `NotApplied`. Worse, `classify_row`'s `ProbeKind::ConfiguredOnly`
branch maps a guard-reported `NotApplicable` straight to `LayerAttestationStatus::NotApplicable`
regardless of the registry row's own `expected: true` cell for this arm — so a walk that legitimately
ran (had at least one ancestor to consider) but found ZERO owned ancestors to grant on causes the row
to VANISH from the decision entirely on an `expected: true` / `outcome: Abort` row, exactly the
green-by-absence failure mode this phase exists to close. This is the same predicate-width defect
class as CR-01 — a guard that structurally cannot report a negative on an input where one is warranted.

The fix distinguishes "the walk had nothing to consider at all" (genuinely `NotApplicable` — e.g. the
walk target is itself a filesystem root) from "the walk ran and granted zero owned ancestors"
(`PartiallyApplied` — a real, non-vacuous fact that must downgrade the session's claim, not disappear),
while leaving "the walk granted at least one ancestor" as `Applied`, unchanged from today.

Purpose: every reachable value a caller-reported coverage type can take must correspond to a genuinely
distinct real-world state, and an `expected: true` row must never silently drop out of the decision.
Output: `dacl_guard.rs` with a `walked` field and 3-state `application()` on both ancestor guards, a
corrected `mod.rs` doc comment, and a new gate-level regression test in `launch.rs`.
</objective>
