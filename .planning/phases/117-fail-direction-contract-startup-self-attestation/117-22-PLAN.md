<objective>
Close WR-06: `DaemonAttestationDecision`'s doc comment (added by Plan 117-17) asserts
`DaemonDaclGuard::apply` "has no partial-success return, so there is no partial-coverage state for a
downgrade arm to represent" — this is factually false. Pass 1 has a real skip arm (an owned-check
`Ok(false)` that logs and continues without recording anything) and pass 3 breaks early at the first
non-owned ancestor; `granted_write_access()` cannot express either. The deletion of `ProceedDowngraded`
is a defensible call (the fail direction is safe — under-granting reduces access, never reduces
confinement), but the comment's stated reasoning is wrong, and no cross-mirror test exists to catch a
future divergence between the CLI's three-state `AttestationDecision` and the daemon's two-state
mirror.

Also close WR-11: the existing `daemon_attestation_decision_is_deliberately_two_state` test parses the
FIRST occurrence of the literal `"enum DaemonAttestationDecision {"` in its own `include_str!`'d
source — a literal that necessarily appears twice (once at the real declaration, once inside the
test's own `.split(...)` call), so the test's own written form is fragile in exactly the way NR3-07
already flagged for a sibling test.

Purpose: the daemon's attestation-decision surface must state its true rationale, stay provably in
sync with the CLI's decision shape, and its own discovery tests must not be blind by the same
first-match fragility this codebase has hit twice before.
Output: `crates/nono-cli/src/agent_daemon/launch.rs` with a corrected doc comment, a hardened
two-state test, a new cross-mirror subset test, and a new exhaustive-match behavioral test.
</objective>
