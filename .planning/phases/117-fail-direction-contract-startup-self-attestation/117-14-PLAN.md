<objective>
Close NR3-02 — CR-14's "report guard effect, not guard construction" fix was applied to
`AppliedLabelsGuard` and `AppliedDaclGrantsGuard`, but two structurally identical guards were left
behind: `mod.rs::applied_layers()` derives `dacl_ancestor_traverse` and `dacl_ancestor_read_attrs`
from `self._applied_ancestor_traverse.is_some()` / `self._applied_ancestor_read_attrs.is_some()`,
and `execution_runtime.rs` sets `package_sid: Some(..)` unconditionally on every Windows launch —
so both `Option`s are constructed `Some` on every shipped `DirectCli` arm and both rows are the
literal constant `Applied`. Neither row can ever report a negative in a shipped build, even though
both registry rows carry `outcome: Abort`.

Purpose: a registry row that structurally cannot deny is the exact "reports enforcing while a
layer is silently inert" failure mode CINT-02 exists to eliminate — it just happens to currently
have no live trigger, which is precisely what made the SAME shape invisible in `MandatoryIntegrityLabel`
before CR-14.
Output: `AppliedAncestorTraverseGuard`/`AppliedAncestorReadAttributesGuard` gain a real coverage
accessor (mirroring `DaclGrantCoverage::application()`'s shape exactly), and `mod.rs` consumes it
instead of `Option::is_some()`.
</objective>
