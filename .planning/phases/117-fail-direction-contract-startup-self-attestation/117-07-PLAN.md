<objective>
Add a compiled-out AppContainer force-unavailable hook to both places that construct
`SECURITY_CAPABILITIES`/AppContainer profiles: `nono-shell-broker`'s own spawn of the real Low-IL
child, and `agent_daemon/launch.rs`'s independent, structurally-separate implementation. Since
`nono-shell-broker` is a standalone binary crate with no `[features]` block today, this plan adds
one — not an extension of an existing block, genuinely new crate plumbing (RESEARCH §E, PATTERNS
"Shared Patterns" confirm this explicitly).

Purpose: RESEARCH finding 2 is that nono-cli's own suspended-spawn window never sees the real
confined child on the broker arm — the actual AppContainer child is spawned inside the broker's own
process. A force-unavailable hook at nono-cli's site alone cannot exercise this layer's real
fail-direction; the hook has to live where the AppContainer is actually applied.
Output: new `[features]` block in `nono-shell-broker`; two force-unavailable hooks.
</objective>
