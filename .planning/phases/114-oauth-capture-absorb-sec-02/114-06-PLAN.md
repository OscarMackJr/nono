<objective>
Wire Plan 114-05's `relay_response_with_capture()` helper into the remaining two of the three
`reverse.rs` response-relay sites — `handle_spiffe_route` (site 2, direct JWT-SVID bearer path)
and `handle_spiffe_assertion_credential` (site 3, RFC 7523 jwt-bearer exchange path) — and prove
each independently, per WR-13's lesson that "one path of six/three ignoring a gate" is the exact
defect shape a shared assertion across sites would mask.

Purpose: a capture-declared route's SPIFFE-authenticated request-side auth mechanism must not
determine whether its RESPONSE gets buffered and rewritten — the enforcement point applies
regardless of which of the three credential-dispatch paths served the request. Without this
plan, a route configured with BOTH `spiffe` (request-side auth) AND `capture` (response-side
rewrite) would leak the real token through sites 2 or 3 even though site 1 is protected.
Output: sites 2 and 3 both call `relay_response_with_capture()`; 2 new independent tests (one per
site) proving the rewrite fires; a regression grep proving no 4th hand-duplicated loop exists.
</objective>
