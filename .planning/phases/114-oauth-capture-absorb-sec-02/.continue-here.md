<context>
The strategic point of this phase: operator decision D-01r retracted a formal DECLINE of upstream's
OAuth capture and replaced it with an ADAPT, specifically on the promise that the fork builds its
OWN response buffer-and-rewrite enforcement point. The investigation behind that retraction
overturned the original premise — the fork's reverse proxy already has plaintext visibility into
upstream responses, so TLS interception was never required for token endpoints reachable as
configured routes. The real blocker was buffering, not visibility. `114-05` IS that enforcement

control-flow audit matters more than its size suggests: if a capture-declared route can fall
through to the unbuffered 8 KiB streaming loop, the phase ships the exact fail-open half-feature
its own goal forbids verbatim, and the retraction was unjustified.

technical theme is that plan-time predictions of blast radius have been wrong in every single
plan, always in the same direction (under-predicting), and always caught only by the full
