<objective>
Close the two dev-host-verifiable gaps in `scripts/verify-authenticode.ps1` that
`101-SIGN03-SMOKE-VERDICT.md` (Finding C) and `104-RESEARCH.md` (Primary Investigation +
Pattern 1/Pattern 2) identified during the live 2026-07-03 smoke-run diagnosis: (1) the
D-04 diagnostic-flush gap, where `Write-Error` terminates the script under CI's forced
`$ErrorActionPreference='Stop'` before `Write-ChainDiagnostic` ever runs, and (2) the
Online-mode chain classifier's blind spot, where a genuine `UntrustedRoot` can be masked
behind transient-looking `RevocationStatusUnknown`/`OfflineRevocation` flags on a
truncated (root-omitting) chain, causing 3 wasted retry attempts on a condition that can
never resolve via retry.

Neither fix touches the fail-closed `Status -ne 'Valid'` gate — both are strictly
additive legibility/classification corrections, byte-for-byte preserving the existing
pass/fail contract (SIGN-02's own invariant, re-confirmed by this plan's own grep-checked
acceptance criteria).

Purpose: makes the *next* Trusted Signing smoke/release verify failure (if the external
root-propagation blocker recurs, or for any future failure class) fully legible in the CI
log on the first attempt, and stops the retry loop from burning ~14s of backoff on a
failure class that provably cannot resolve via retry.

Output: `scripts/verify-authenticode.ps1` hardened with both fixes;
`scripts/tests/test_verify_authenticode.ps1` extended with 2 new regression-guard cases
(6 total); full harness green.
</objective>

| Caller's ambient `$ErrorActionPreference` <-> shared helper's internal control flow | `Assert-TrustedSignature` is dot-sourced into 3+ different call sites (release.yml x2, trusted-signing-smoke.yml, Phase 103's gates) each with a different ambient error-handling context; the helper must not assume any particular caller preference |
