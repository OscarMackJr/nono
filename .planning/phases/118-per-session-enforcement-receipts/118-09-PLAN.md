<objective>
Give an operator a way to actually retrieve and verify receipts (D-09's "same-trust-domain
governance consumer" — the operator, not a third party) via a `nono receipt list|show|verify`
command family mirroring the shipped `AuditCommands` shape verbatim (D-10). Prove RCPT-03's
four-state distinguishability with a discovery-based test, not a manual read of the output.

Purpose: without this, everything Plans 118-01 through 118-08 built is unretrievable data sitting
in a directory — this plan is what makes RCPT-02 and RCPT-03 operationally true, not just
structurally true.
Output: `ReceiptCommands` in `cli.rs`; `receipt_commands.rs`; `app_runtime.rs` dispatch wiring;
a four-state rendering discovery test.
</objective>
